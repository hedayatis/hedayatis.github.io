Profile

Operations researcher with around eight years of professional experience. I take operational problems that no off-the-shelf tool covers — capacity, inspection, scheduling and routing under uncertainty — formulate them, build the algorithm that makes them solvable at realistic size, and deliver the result as an application the people who own the decision can run themselves. Since 2022 I have done this as a project researcher with Ghent University and Flanders Make on an industrial partner’s own data: the simulation and analytics tool I built there provided the key evidence behind Flanders Make’s decision to continue funding the programme. Before that, three years in planning, industrial engineering and process consulting, presenting to boards and management teams.

Research focus: optimization under uncertainty for production and logistics planning — two-stage stochastic programming, exact and decomposition methods, and learning from data with provable guarantees. Three journal articles, including Computers & Operations Research (2026); reviewer for three international OR journals.

  • Solved what the solver could not. Formulated inspection-station and capacity allocation as integer programmes, then built two Lagrangian relaxation approaches with greedy repair and hill-climbing search that return near-optimal plans with bounded gaps where the commercial solver stalls at the time limit; on the largest benchmark, runtime fell from 418.50 s to 23.31 s with the reported gap below 8%. Benchmarked against the exact solver and a problem-specific genetic algorithm; published in Computers & Operations Research (2026).
  • Made an intractable case exact. Derived an exact dynamic program for the uncapacitated case: identical optima on all 10,000 tested instances and an 8.6× average speedup at 100 stages, verified against an independent branch-and-bound implementation.
  • Showed what modelling uncertainty is worth. On an independent holdout sample, the stochastic inspection plan cost 9.3% less than expected-value planning; a certified scenario reduction then cut 1,000 scenarios to 50 without giving back the tested out-of-sample result.
  • Planned under uncertain supply. Multi-period disassembly planning and scheduling with uncertain return volumes and material condition: two-stage stochastic programming, sample average approximation with statistical gap bounds and scenario reduction, benchmarked against a deterministic model to quantify what the stochastic plan is actually worth.
  • Priced a model’s accuracy. Modelled how an imperfect condition-triage classifier propagates error into downstream throughput and cost, turning “is this model good enough?” into a figure a business can decide on.
  • Made a learned optimizer replace a hand-tuned one. Trained a PPO agent to learn Lagrangian multiplier updates in place of hand-tuned subgradient schedules.

Research contributions

Scenario reduction

Stochastic programming and certified scenario reduction

  • Two-stage stochastic integer programs for inspection allocation, solved by sample average approximation and L-shaped / Benders decomposition with statistical optimality-gap estimation.
  • A scenario-reduction certificate: a computable bound on the error of a reduced scenario set that holds uniformly over all first-stage decisions, with stratified sampling, robust variants and scalability studies.
  • A contextual extension in which scenario reduction and plans depend on observed covariates.
Current line

Learning decision-dependent feasible sets

  • Two-stage programs whose recourse domain depends on the first-stage decision and on an unknown contextual parameter: proved existence, lower semicontinuity and stability, and showed that a single-sided indicator formulation is exact for threshold activation.
  • Estimated the domain with honest random forests and nearest neighbours, with finite-sample optimality bounds, activation recovery under a margin condition, and a calibration that certifies an inner domain; synthetic study over 720 data sets.
Exact methods

Allocation and scheduling

  • Integer programming formulations for inspection-station allocation with delayed inspection, strengthened by valid inequalities, lazy cuts and warm starts; two Lagrangian relaxations with dual-guided repair and local search.
  • An exact dynamic-programming algorithm for the uncapacitated variant, with correctness proof.
  • Stochastic lot-sizing and scheduling for quality-differentiated disassembly (logic-based Benders).
Logistics

Logistics and warehousing · with TU/e and Cardiff University

  • Joint order batching and picker routing with alternative pick locations, modelled as a clustered generalized vehicle routing problem; two MILP formulations and a cost-matrix rule for rectangular warehouses; instances up to 180 nodes.
  • Re-supplying autonomous mobile parcel lockers: meeting-point selection and truck routing with multiple time windows and time-dependent parking costs, solved by cluster-based simulated annealing on a new benchmark set generated from Solomon data and validated against CPLEX.

Problems solved, and what changed

Solved what the commercial solver could not

Integer programme for inspection-station allocation with deferrable inspection under limited capacity, then two Lagrangian relaxation approaches with greedy repair heuristics and hill-climbing local search — returning near-optimal plans with bounded optimality gaps on instances where an exact commercial solver stalled at the time limit. Benchmarked against both the exact solver and a problem-specific genetic algorithm. Computers & Operations Research 194, 107588 (2026)

Scaled a warehouse routing formulation

Clustered and generalized vehicle routing formulation for joint order batching and picker routing with alternative pick locations in mixed-shelves warehouses: two MILP formulations plus a general rule for cost-matrix construction in rectangular environments, solving instances up to 180 nodes. IMA Journal of Management Mathematics 35(2), 241–265 (2023)

Built a benchmark where none existed

Cluster-based simulated annealing — k-means clustering with six neighbourhood operators — for re-supplying autonomous mobile parcel lockers under multiple time windows and time-dependent parking costs. Generated a new benchmark instance set from Solomon data and validated against IBM CPLEX. Future Transportation 4(4), 1266–1296 (2024)

Showed what the stochastic treatment was worth

Two-stage stochastic disassembly scheduling with a coupled recourse stage under shared procurement cost, benchmarking a decomposition-inspired simulated annealing heuristic with an embedded MIP evaluating recourse against both the full extensive-form MIP and a deterministic model. IWLS 2025 — 15th International Workshop on Lot Sizing

Also on the record

Turned an intractable formulation into a polynomial-time routine

Derived an exact dynamic-programming algorithm for the uncapacitated case of the inspection-allocation problem.

Put a number on what a model's accuracy is worth

Modelled how an imperfect condition-triage classifier propagates error into downstream throughput and cost — converting "is this model accurate enough?" into a defensible figure and a rational basis for investing in a better one. Decision quality rather than metric quality.

Made a learned optimizer beat a hand-tuned one

Trained a PPO agent in a custom Gym environment to learn Lagrangian multiplier update policies, replacing hand-tuned subgradient schedules. Co-supervised an M.Sc. thesis extending this into a decoupled GNN + RL framework for the generalized assignment problem, separating learned search direction from learned step-size control — reduced multiplier oscillation with better final bounds.
